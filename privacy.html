Privacy Policy

Channel Data Local · Last updated: November 1, 2026

What the app accesses

  • Read-only YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data for the channel owned by the signed-in user.
  • A read-only list of that channel's videos and their details.

The app never uploads, edits, deletes or publishes anything, and it does not request access to revenue data.

How the data is used and stored

Data is downloaded only to the channel owner's own computer and is used only by the owner to review the channel's performance. Access credentials are stored on that computer with owner-only file permissions. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and none of it is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Data Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local files removes all downloaded data.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/aaronyi97/channel-data-local/issues.
